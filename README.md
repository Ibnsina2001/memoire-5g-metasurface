# Applications of Metasurface Structures on Antenna Design for 5G Communications

Master's thesis (mémoire de fin d'études) — **Mohamed El-Bachir El-Ibrahimi University, Bordj Bou Arreridj, Algeria**, Faculty of Science and Technology, Department of Electronics, speciality Telecommunications System.

- **Author:** Azzi Ibnsina Faiz
- **Supervisor:** Idris Messaoudene
- **Defended:** 30 June 2025 (public defence, jury: S. Titouni, M. Belazzoug, I. Messaoudene)

## Abstract

This thesis explores two concepts in modern electromagnetics — **metasurfaces (MTS)** and **characteristic mode analysis (CMA)** — and applies them to antenna design for 5G communications. Two antenna configurations were designed and analysed by simulation, each studied with and without a metasurface: a low-profile broadband antenna with multimode resonance, and a low-profile wide-beam antenna based on the complementary-sources concept. The combination of MTS and CMA produced compact, efficient antennas with better matching, stable gain and controlled radiation patterns.

## Contents

- **Chapter I** — Theory: metasurface types and applications; CMA methodologies and applications.
- **Chapter II** — Two simulated antenna designs, with without/with metasurface comparison (return loss, gain, radiation patterns, CMA).
- **Full thesis:** `memoire-azzi-m2-stpfe.pdf` (69 pages, English, with French and Arabic abstracts).

## Key results (simulation)

- Wide-beam design: S11 **-26 dB** (vs -14 dB without MTS); peak gain **9 dBi** (vs 6.9 dBi); resonance shifted **17 GHz to 6 GHz** (miniaturisation); sidelobe level **-8.1 dB**; 5.5% bandwidth (6.3-6.47 GHz) covering Sub-6 GHz.
- Work is simulation-based; no fabricated prototype or measured results.
